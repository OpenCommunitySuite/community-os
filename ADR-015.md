# ADR-015 — Authentication, authorization, secrets and technical/security audit

**Статус:** Proposed / Предложено
**Язык документа:** русский

---

## Контекст

ADR-001–ADR-011 зафиксировали предметную идентичность, полномочия, допустимость, историю и интеграционные границы. ADR-012 определил platform-global User Account, optional Subject Identity Anchor, Community-owned Subject, Control Plane и Community lifecycle. ADR-013 установил Application/Domain boundary, а ADR-014 — trusted Community context, RLS defense in depth и persistence encryption boundary.

Техническая security architecture должна реализовать эти различия, не превращая аутентификацию или роль доступа в предметное полномочие, Platform Admin — в global tenant superuser, workload — в Subject, а Security Audit — в universal domain history.

## Область решения

ADR определяет semantic boundaries для Authentication Methods, factors/challenges, OIDC, Account lifecycle/link corrections, technical authorization, grants/scopes, privileged contexts, Workload Identities, integration credentials, Secrets, cryptographic isolation, revocation/recovery и Security Audit.

ADR не выбирает IAM framework, protocol library, password/OTP/token algorithms, schema, KMS/provider, exact MFA policy, database roles, audit storage/SIEM или operational runbooks.

## Связь с ADR-001–ADR-014

- Account, Technical Identity и Subject различны; automated mechanism не становится Subject (ADR-001/010).
- Identity/Accounts/Access остаются cross-cutting responsibility, не новым bounded context (ADR-002).
- Assurance/access/security configuration сохраняет явные owner, scope и history без global configuration hierarchy (ADR-003–005).
- Finance, Resource, Governance и Documents сохраняют context-owned admissibility/history (ADR-006–009).
- External identity/credential не создаёт Subject, Power или recognized domain fact (ADR-011).
- Account/Anchor/Community Subject, Entitlement и lifecycle различны (ADR-012).
- Application coordinates checks; Domain owns admissibility; Web и Worker соблюдают одинаковые boundaries (ADR-013).
- Community Scope trusted explicitly; RLS не является authorization; privileged persistence identity отделена от normal runtime (ADR-014).

## Решение

### 1. Identity и authentication taxonomy

Не объединяются универсальной IAM/domain сущностью:

```text
User Account
≠ Subject
≠ Subject Identity Anchor
≠ Technical Identity
≠ Workload Identity
≠ qualified External Identity
≠ Authentication Method
≠ Access Role / Access Right
≠ Entitlement
≠ Power / Representation / Position / Membership / Competence
≠ domain admissibility
```

**Authentication Method** — связанное с технической идентичностью средство аутентификации. Один User Account может иметь несколько methods; каждая конкретная qualified authentication identity связана максимум с одним Account. Method можно добавить или отозвать независимо от Account, не уничтожая Account/history.

Email, phone или name сами по себе не являются достаточным identity key или основанием linkage.

### 2. Password, factors, challenges и assurance

Built-in password authentication является baseline. Architecture расширяема для дополнительных methods/factors, включая TOTP, passkeys/WebAuthn и future mechanisms.

Следует семантически различать, не требуя универсальной persistence model:

- **Authentication Factor** — категория evidence в authentication decision;
- **Authentication Challenge** — short-lived proof, запрошенный для authentication/recovery;
- **Challenge Delivery Channel** — transport одноразового challenge/code;
- **Recovery Method/Channel** — средство восстановления контроля;
- **Assurance Policy** — context/risk-sensitive policy допустимых methods/factors/challenges и step-up.

OTP value не является password. TOTP создаётся независимо от online delivery и может быть дополнительным factor; приложения класса Google Authenticator являются совместимым примером, не dependency.

Email, SMS и Telegram могут доставлять одноразовый challenge. Они имеют разные risk/assurance profiles и не считаются автоматически эквивалентными TOTP. Канал, используемый также для recovery, не становится автоматически независимым strong second factor. Telegram binding/chat identity не является Subject и не создаёт Community access; compromised binding/channel revocable.

Security-sensitive и privileged operations, включая support elevation и break-glass, должны поддерживать assurance stronger than ordinary authentication согласно explicit risk-based policy. Конкретный factor или combination не фиксируется.

### 3. OIDC и external identities

Trusted OIDC authentication может provision User Account. Qualified external identity основывается на issuer/provider и stable provider-subject semantics; email остаётся claim, не identity key. Deployment может применять invite gate.

External login или IdP claims не создают Community access, Subject/Anchor link, Membership, Position, Representation, Power, Access Right, Voting Right или domain admissibility. Claims не создают Domain Power напрямую. Допустимое claims-to-technical-grants mapping бывает только explicit, scoped и managed.

### 4. Account lifecycle и link corrections

Различаются Account lifecycle, method lifecycle, suspension/disable, deletion request/process, recovery, link correction, prospective replacement, Anchor correction и destructive merge/split.

Silent deduplication и automatic email/phone/name linking запрещены. Used identity link не silently rebound. Correction явна, traceable и не reattributes past actions. Prospective replacement меняет future applicability, не историю. Community Subjects остаются distinct и Community-owned. General destructive Account/Anchor merge/split не входит baseline.

### 5. Layered authorization

Conceptual evaluation:

```text
authenticated Technical Identity
→ active Account or Workload Identity
→ trusted Platform/Community execution context
→ lifecycle and Entitlement execution gates
→ current Access Rights in qualified Scope
→ represented Subject/basis where applicable
→ context-owned Power/Representation/Competence/rules/state
→ domain admissibility
→ allow only if every mandatory layer allows
```

Technical authorization отвечает за техническую допустимость capability/read в scope. Domain owner определяет admissibility domain action. Application coordinates mandatory checks. Mandatory negative domain result cannot be overridden. Domain Power не предоставляет technical access; Access Right не создаёт Power; Entitlement enables product capability, но не создаёт Access Right/Power.

RLS остаётся persistence defense in depth, не authorization. Cached/token/projection state не может самостоятельно grant current-sensitive operation. Universal Policy Engine и domain `Authorization Decision` не требуются.

### 6. Access Rights, Roles и grants

Access Rights — stable technical capabilities. Access Role группирует Rights и не является domain role, Position, Membership, Representation, Power или Voting Right.

Baseline поддерживает role-based, deliberate direct и deliberate time-limited grants. Для grant должны быть определимы authorized grantor, grantee Account/Technical Identity, Right, qualified Scope, validity interval where applicable, revocation state и security-audit attribution.

Generic DENY, nested roles, role inheritance, universal hierarchical RBAC/ACL не являются baseline. Grant expiration/revocation прекращает future technical access, не изменяя Domain Power или historical actions.

### 7. Qualified Scope

Minimal qualified Scope contract может выражать kind, stable qualified reference и Community/platform boundary. Он не создаёт universal Resource hierarchy/tree/entity. Object, Governance Body, Accounting Point, Document, self-service и assigned-work scope semantics принадлежат owning context. Knowledge of identifier grants no access.

### 8. Acting on behalf

Where domain semantics require, execution context supplies distinct technical identity/account, actual Subject, represented Subject, Representation/Power basis, Position/Competence and Community/organ context. Support/workload/bootstrap technical actor может быть attributable без fake Subject только когда domain semantics не требует Subject. Support context не заменяет domain Representation.

### 9. Platform administration, support elevation и break-glass

Normal Platform Admin не имеет automatic tenant-content access.

**Support Elevation** — explicit, scoped, purpose-bound, time-limited, revocable access под собственной Technical Identity оператора с mandatory Security Audit. Community delegation/approval может требоваться policy where practical, но не является universal prerequisite. Support access не создаёт Subject/Representation/Power. Writes зависят от purpose/policy и никогда не bypass mandatory domain admissibility.

**Break-glass** — exceptional explicitly activated capability с strong assurance, limited scope/time, explicit reason, audit, termination/revocation и post-event review. Как emergency route он может bypass ordinary support approval, но не attribution, audit, scope, assurance или meaningful mandatory domain invariants.

Normal Platform Admin, Support Elevation, break-glass, impersonation и domain Representation различны.

Direct “login as user” и silent Account/Subject impersonation запрещены. Future read-only effective-view simulation допустима только явно как simulation: без commands/actions от user identity и без подмены Representation/Power.

### 10. Community bootstrap/admin recovery

Technical Community Admin может существовать без Subject linkage и не становится chairman, owner/member, legal representative, Position, Representation или Power. Domain operation, требующая Subject/basis/Power, не разрешается только administrative access.

Поддерживаются multiple Community admins, explicit grants и revocation. Loss of all admins использует exceptional verified recovery под собственной Technical Identity recovery operator: без impersonation/fake Subject, с explicit grant change и Security Audit. Exact identity proofing/legal authority deferred.

### 11. Workload Identities и privileged persistence

Worker, scheduler, integration adapter, telemetry importer, migration, backup/restore, relocation и DNS/certificate manager не User Accounts и не Subjects. Они используют distinct Workload/Technical Identities с least privilege, explicit purpose, Platform/Community Scope, revocable credentials и auditable attribution.

Privileged maintenance identities отделены от normal runtime и активируются для controlled purpose. Platform Admin не становится PostgreSQL superuser/RLS-bypass identity автоматически.

### 12. Integration credentials

```text
integration configuration
≠ credential metadata/reference
≠ Secret material
≠ qualified external identity
≠ Subject
```

Это применяется к bank, Telegram, SMTP/email, SMS, OIDC, MQTT, BAS, PowerDNS/ACME, S3, PostgreSQL и future APIs. Credential имеет owner, purpose, environment, Community/platform scope и lifecycle. Universal External System domain entity не вводится.

### 13. Secrets

Secret metadata/reference, protected Secret material, ordinary configuration и cryptographic key material различаются. Используется opaque Secret reference/handle. Metadata может включать owner, purpose, scope, environment, version и lifecycle/status.

Material не normally readable и не возвращается plaintext через ordinary APIs; он предоставляется только authorized runtime use. Поддерживаются versioning, activation, rotation, temporary overlap и revocation.

Controlled export/recovery для self-host portability, migration, DR или store replacement является exceptional, explicitly authorized, purpose-scoped, minimized и audited process, а не “show secret”. Secret material никогда не попадает в Security Audit, logs или domain history/provenance.

### 14. Cryptographic isolation

Architecture поддерживает hierarchical/purpose/risk-based compromise isolation: environment, purpose и Community-specific isolation where warranted, rotation, revocation, recovery и containment. Один undifferentiated global master key отвергается; mandatory key-per-record также не требуется. Envelope/hierarchical approach conceptually compatible, но algorithms, exact hierarchy и provider не выбираются.

Relocation re-resolves authorized consumers без placement-dependent secret identity. Deletion/backup/recovery не должны уничтожать keys, пока они нужны для authorized recovery retained encrypted data.

### 15. Sessions, revocation и revalidation

Authentication/application session, access/refresh capability, API/workload credential и one-time recovery capability имеют разные semantics.

Current-sensitive operations revalidate authoritative authorization/admissibility/lifecycle state. Old session, token claim, cache or projection alone cannot preserve revoked authority. Less-sensitive operations may use policy-defined bounded staleness.

Technical Right revocation и domain Power/Representation expiration проверяются соответствующими owners. Persistent work revalidates relevant state at appropriate execution or irreversible-action boundary. ADR-016 определяет job/invalidation/retry mechanics.

### 16. Security Audit и security views

Security Audit — append-oriented technical contract, distinct from domain history/provenance, operational logs, metrics and traces. Safe envelope may include event kind/time, Technical Identity, Account/Workload Identity, real Subject only where applicable, scope, target, reason/ticket, correlation, result and safe before/after metadata.

Audit охватывает security-sensitive authentication/recovery/method/session, grants/admin, elevation/break-glass, Secret/key/credential lifecycle, privileged persistence и security configuration operations. Password, OTP, session/API token, Secret or key material never recorded.

Audit access least-privileged and itself auditable; minimization/redaction and retention/access policy required. Exact retention/storage/SIEM/tamper-resistance mechanism deferred.

Five purposes remain distinct:

1. internal Platform Security Audit;
2. Community Admin filtered security view;
3. user personal security activity;
4. operational telemetry;
5. domain history/provenance.

Personal activity is filtered projection, not raw audit, and may show login/session, Authentication Method, MFA/recovery and relevant Account security events. Exact device/IP/location/privacy presentation deferred.

### 17. Community lifecycle и Entitlement

Lifecycle execution gate, Entitlement, Access Right, Domain Power и domain history distinct. Suspended/archived Community denies normal mutations/background effects by default while explicitly authorized read/export/recovery paths may remain.

Lifecycle restriction does not erase Membership/Power, rewrite history, delete Finance facts or reattribute actions. Final deletion follows ADR-014. Detailed state/operation matrix deferred.

## Validation scenarios

- Compromised password/method/session can be revoked; sensitive actions revalidate current state.
- Compromised email/SMS/Telegram channel does not become proof of Subject/Power; assurance policy reflects channel risk.
- OIDC takeover/email change does not change qualified identity by email or grant domain authority.
- Wrong Subject/Anchor link is corrected traceably without historical reattribution.
- Revoked Right/cache or expired Power/session cannot independently authorize a sensitive action.
- Client-substituted Community ID fails trusted scope checks and remains constrained by RLS.
- Malicious Platform Admin has no default tenant access; support elevation is scoped/audited and cannot impersonate.
- Abandoned support elevation expires/revokes; break-glass is exceptional and reviewed.
- Lost Community admins use verified own-identity recovery, not impersonation.
- Compromised workload/integration credential is purpose-scoped and independently revocable.
- Secret export cannot be normal reveal; secret/key compromise is contained by scope and rotation.
- Relocation/suspension causes persistent work to re-resolve/revalidate before irreversible effects.
- Privileged restore uses separate attributable identity.
- Personal activity cannot expose raw internal audit details.
- Recovery channel cannot silently bypass assurance required by operation risk.

## Архитектурные инварианты

1. Account, Subject, Anchor, Technical/Workload/External Identity и Authentication Method distinct.
2. No universal Identity, Principal, Actor or IAM superentity.
3. Multiple methods/account allowed; qualified identity binds to at most one Account.
4. Contacts/names do not establish linkage.
5. Authentication creates no Community access, Subject, Power or admissibility.
6. Links are not silently rebound; corrections preserve past attribution.
7. OTP is challenge/code, not password; delivery channels are not assurance-equivalent.
8. Privileged operations can require stronger risk-based assurance.
9. Access Role groups Rights only; Right and Power distinct.
10. Grants are qualified/scoped/revocable/auditable.
11. No universal Resource hierarchy or mandatory hierarchical RBAC.
12. Domain owner owns admissibility; mandatory denial cannot be overridden.
13. Entitlement/lifecycle/technical/domain gates are distinct and conjunctive where mandatory.
14. RLS is defense-in-depth, not authorization.
15. Stale state cannot grant current-sensitive operation.
16. Acting-on-behalf does not collapse technical actor, Subject and basis.
17. Platform Admin has no default tenant-content access.
18. Support elevation, break-glass, impersonation and Representation distinct.
19. Direct Account/Subject impersonation prohibited.
20. Bootstrap and Workload actors need no fake Subject.
21. Web and Worker obey the same authorization/domain boundaries.
22. Secrets are separate from configuration; normal plaintext retrieval prohibited.
23. Secret material never enters audit/log/provenance.
24. Cryptographic isolation contains compromise; no undifferentiated global master key.
25. Security Audit, personal/Community views, telemetry and domain history distinct.
26. Security Audit is technical, not universal domain Audit Event.
27. Lifecycle gate does not erase domain relations/facts/history.

## Последствия и риски

Положительно: preserves domain authority, supports federation/MFA evolution, isolates tenant support and credentials, enables revocation and explainable privileged actions. Цена: assurance/grant/recovery/audit policies and operational secret/key lifecycle require disciplined implementation. Main risks are weak recovery, stale authorization, overly broad support and secret/audit leakage.

## Отклонённые альтернативы

- Account/Subject/Identity superentity;
- authentication or IdP claims as domain authority;
- email/phone auto-linking and silent dedup/rebind;
- RBAC role as Power/Position/Voting Right;
- universal Resource/ACL hierarchy and mandatory nested roles/DENY;
- default Platform Admin tenant access or permanent emergency superuser;
- direct login-as-user impersonation;
- fake System/Worker Subject;
- stale token/projection as authoritative grant;
- Secrets as ordinary readable configuration or normal “show secret”;
- undifferentiated global master key or mandatory key-per-record;
- raw Security Audit as personal activity/domain history;
- RLS as authorization;
- lifecycle/Entitlement as destructive change of Domain Power/history.

## Намеренно отложенные решения

### ADR-016

Persistent-job revalidation mechanics, retries, invalidation messages, durable messaging and integration execution mechanics.

### ADR-017

Secret/audit service availability, security monitoring/alerts, privileged runbooks, recovery/rotation drills and operational enforcement.

### Implementation Baseline

Password hashing; OTP algorithms/TTL/length/rate limits; token/cookie/session mechanism; OIDC library; exact MFA combinations; authorization engine/storage/cache invalidation; KMS/Vault/provider; algorithms/exact key hierarchy; PostgreSQL roles/grants; audit storage/tamper-resistance implementation.

### Policy/future

Identity-proofing evidence; exact privileged-factor policy; support write/approval and notification matrix; audit retention durations; detailed Community lifecycle matrix; Account merge feature; exact Account deletion consequences under retention/domain requirements.

## Совместимость

ADR-015 implements ADR-010 identity/power/access semantics without revising them; preserves ADR-012 Account/Anchor/Subject and admin/lifecycle boundaries; follows ADR-013 Application/Domain and Web/Worker boundaries; and complements ADR-014 trusted Community context/RLS/persistence security without granting ordinary bypass. Domain histories and rules of ADR-001–ADR-009 remain owned by their contexts, while ADR-011 integration identities/credentials remain qualified and non-domain.
